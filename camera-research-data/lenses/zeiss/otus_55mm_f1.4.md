# Zeiss Otus 55mm f/1.4 — Lens Profile

**Full name:** Zeiss Otus 1.4/55 Apo Distagon T*  
**Mount:** Canon EF and Nikon F  
**Focal length:** 55mm  
**Maximum aperture:** f/1.4  
**Minimum aperture:** f/16  
**Construction:** 12 elements in 10 groups, APO design  
**Era:** 2013–present  
**Weight:** 1,012g (extremely heavy for a 55mm lens)

---

## Visual Identity

The Zeiss Otus 55mm f/1.4 is, by many optical measurements, the finest 55mm lens ever made. It was designed with a single goal: maximum optical performance on high-resolution sensors (36MP+ Sony, Nikon, Canon sensors). It achieves this through an apochromatic design that eliminates chromatic aberration across the full spectrum — similar to the Leica APO-Summicron but for a different mount system.

The Otus 55 has a rendering that is often described as "medium format on full-frame" — the combination of extraordinary resolution, colour accuracy, and tonal depth produces images that look more like scanned 4×5 film than typical digital photography.

Where the Leica APO-Summicron has analogue warmth in its rendering (Leica's colour matrix), the Zeiss Otus is more analytical — cooler, more scientifically precise. It is the German optical tradition applied to its logical extreme.

---

## Rendering Character

**At f/1.4:** Excellent. The Otus, unlike most f/1.4 lenses, is very well-corrected wide open. No significant spherical aberration. No chromatic aberration. Clean, precise subject rendering at the widest aperture.

**Colour transmission:** Neutral with the characteristic Zeiss T* coating signature — very slightly cool, with exceptional contrast and colour separation. Colours are clean and differentiated — shadow detail and midtone colour distinction are exceptional.

**Bokeh:** Very smooth at f/1.4. The Otus's APO design produces subject-background separation that is similar in quality to the Leica Summilux — clean background dissolution without the painterly impressionism of the Summilux.

**Micro-contrast:** Exceptional. Fine texture rendering is among the best of any 55mm lens. Comparable to the Leica APO-Summicron.

## Combination Notes

The Otus 55 is typically used on high-resolution bodies — Nikon D800/D850/Z7, Sony A7R series, Canon 5DS. The combination of 36–50MP sensor and the Otus's resolution delivers the most technically detailed images possible in the 55mm format.
