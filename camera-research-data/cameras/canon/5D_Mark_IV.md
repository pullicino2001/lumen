# Canon EOS 5D Mark IV — Camera Profile

**Type:** Digital SLR (DSLR)  
**Sensor:** Full-frame CMOS, 30.4MP  
**Production:** 2016–present  
**ISO Range:** 100–32,000 (expandable to 102,400)  
**Autofocus:** 61-point phase-detect AF  
**Significance:** Canon's professional-grade full-frame DSLR. The standard for event, wedding, and editorial photography for nearly a decade.

---

## Visual Identity

The Canon 5D Mark IV is the workhorse of professional photography. It does not produce the most dramatic or distinctive images — it produces reliable, technically excellent images across an enormous range of conditions. Its colour science is Canon's "house look": warm neutrals, pleasant skin tones, slightly elevated saturation compared to neutral, and a rendering that is technically accurate without being clinical.

Canon's colour science differs fundamentally from Leica's. Canon's JPEG rendering (and to a lesser extent RAW colour matrix) has a warmer, more "consumer-friendly" quality — it makes colours look good rather than accurate. Reds and oranges are particularly rich. Skin tones are warm without being excessively orange. Blues are clean and well-saturated.

The 5D Mark IV is not a camera associated with a particular "look" in the way a Leica or Fuji is. It is associated with *quality* — technically flawless images that can be taken in any direction in post-processing.

---

## Colour Science

**Skin tones:** Warm and pleasant. Canon's skin tone rendering is considered among the best in digital photography — a consistent Canon advantage even as sensor technology has converged.

**Reds/oranges:** Slightly boosted — Canon's colour matrix deliberately enriches warm tones. This reads as warmth and vitality rather than inaccuracy.

**Blues:** Clean and slightly saturated. Sky blues are punchy and rich.

**Greens:** Slightly warm — yellowish rather than cyan. Natural in most contexts.

**Neutrals:** Slightly warm white balance tendency — Canon cameras often bias slightly warm, giving an overall golden quality to images compared to neutral cameras.

---

## Rendering Character

**Contrast:** Medium. The 5D Mark IV's native rendering has moderate contrast — neither flat nor punchy. It provides good latitude for post-processing.

**Highlights:** Good rolloff. Better than many DSLRs at holding highlight detail before clipping. Not as gradual as Leica's current sensors.

**Shadows:** Good recovery. At base ISO, significant shadow information is preserved.

**Sharpness:** High. The 5D Mark IV is a sharp camera. Fine detail is resolved cleanly without obvious processing artefacts.

**Noise character:** Fine and luminance-dominant at moderate ISO. Clean and pleasant through ISO 3200.

## Combination Notes

The 5D Mark IV is most associated with Canon's L-series lenses. The 85mm f/1.2L produces portrait images of exceptional smoothness; the 50mm f/1.4L is an excellent standard prime. With Canon's colour science, portrait work has a reliable warmth that many photographers prefer for weddings and events.
