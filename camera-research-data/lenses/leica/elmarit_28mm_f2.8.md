# Leica Elmarit-M 28mm f/2.8 ASPH — Lens Profile

**Full name:** Leica Elmarit-M 28mm f/2.8 ASPH  
**Mount:** Leica M  
**Focal length:** 28mm  
**Maximum aperture:** f/2.8  
**Minimum aperture:** f/16  
**Minimum focus distance:** 0.7m  
**Construction:** 9 elements in 7 groups, 1 aspherical element  
**Era:** 2006–present  

---

## Visual Identity

The Elmarit 28mm f/2.8 is the compact, accessible wide-angle in the Leica M system. Not as extreme as ultra-wide lenses, not as environmental as a 35mm — 28mm is the perspective of conscious wide-angle choice. Images at 28mm feel slightly wider than "normal" — they place subjects in a world that feels slightly expansive, with more ceiling, more sky, more floor, more sides than a standard lens would include.

At f/2.8, the Elmarit is not a fast lens by modern standards. But the 28mm focal length means that depth of field is extensive even at f/2.8 — at typical street and architectural distances, essentially everything is in focus. This makes it an ideal zone-focus street photography lens: set to f/5.6 and focused to 3m, and a very wide range of the scene is sharp without the need to confirm focus on every frame.

The Elmarit's rendering is clean and controlled — Leica's smallest M-mount wide-angle lens produces images with high sharpness and low distortion that is unusual for a 28mm. Corner sharpness at f/5.6 is exceptional.

---

## Rendering Character

**Perspective:** 28mm creates a particular environmental feeling. Subjects are placed in their surroundings with emphasis — the surrounding environment is as much "subject" as the central point of interest.

**Distortion:** Very low for a 28mm lens. Straight lines remain relatively straight — particularly important for architecture and interior photography.

**Vignetting at f/2.8:** Moderate — approximately 1.5 stops at corners. Noticeable but not extreme.

**Sharpness:** Outstanding from f/4 onwards. Wide open, excellent in the centre with moderate corner softness.

**Colour:** Slightly cool — cooler than the Summilux lenses. Clean and neutral without the warmth of the Summicron or Summilux coating families.

## Combination Notes

The Elmarit 28 on the Q2/Q3 is essentially the Q camera's fixed lens (same focal length, similar character). The Q's Summilux 28 f/1.7 is faster and has slightly more character; the Elmarit is more neutral. For documentary and architectural work, the Elmarit is often preferred for its restraint.
