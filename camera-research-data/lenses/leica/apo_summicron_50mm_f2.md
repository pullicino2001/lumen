# Leica APO-Summicron-M 50mm f/2 ASPH — Lens Profile

**Full name:** Leica APO-Summicron-M 50mm f/2 ASPH  
**Mount:** Leica M  
**Focal length:** 50mm  
**Maximum aperture:** f/2  
**Minimum aperture:** f/16  
**Minimum focus distance:** 0.7m  
**Construction:** 8 elements in 5 groups, 2 aspherical elements, floating element  
**Era:** 2012–present  
**Price:** The most expensive M-mount 50mm lens — typically $8,000–$11,000 new  
**Significance:** Widely considered the technically finest 50mm lens ever made.

---

## Visual Identity

The APO-Summicron is a different class of optical instrument. "APO" stands for apochromatic — a design principle that corrects chromatic aberration across the full visible spectrum, not just the two wavelengths that standard lens designs target. The result is a lens that renders colour with an accuracy that is beyond clinical — it is scientific.

What this means practically: every colour wavelength focuses at precisely the same plane. There is no colour fringing anywhere in the frame at any aperture. Edge transitions are not just sharp — they are *pure*. Greens and reds do not blur slightly beyond the focus plane; they are present with full fidelity right up to the edge of sharpness.

The rendering of the APO-Summicron is unlike any other M-mount lens. It is often described by photographers who use it as feeling like "looking through a window" — the lens is so optically transparent that it contributes almost nothing of its own character. What you see is what was in front of it. This is remarkable and also — for some photographers — slightly unsettling. Images from the APO-Summicron can feel almost too perfect.

---

## Optical Performance

The APO-Summicron is one of the best-performing lenses ever tested by optical review publications:

- **Resolution:** Among the highest recorded for any 50mm lens — resolves detail that most sensors cannot fully capture
- **Chromatic aberration:** Near-zero at all apertures — the APO correction is exceptional
- **Vignetting:** Minimal — approximately 0.5 stops at f/2, negligible by f/2.8
- **Distortion:** Near-zero — images are geometrically precise
- **Field flatness:** Outstanding — the focus plane is flat across the entire image

---

## Rendering Character

**At f/2:** Unlike any other fast lens. The APO-Summicron is as sharp at f/2 as a standard lens is at f/5.6. There are no wide-open compromises — no spherical aberration, no chromatic fringing, no corner softness. Wide open is optimal.

**Colour:** Completely neutral. The APO-Summicron adds no colour cast, no warmth, no saturation enhancement. If you want the image to be warm, you must make it warm in post or via a film stock profile. The lens provides a neutral foundation.

**3D rendering:** Present but different from the Summilux. Rather than the Summilux's romantic subject-lift effect, the APO-Summicron produces a sense of depth through *precision* — each plane of focus is rendered with such clarity that the spatial relationships between them are inherently apparent.

**Micro-contrast:** The highest of any Leica M lens. Fine texture is rendered with extraordinary fidelity. Skin pores, fabric threads, hair — all resolved individually rather than as impressionistic groupings.

---

## The "APO Look"

The APO-Summicron's unique rendering requires a specific approach:

- Its neutrality means it pairs best with careful colour work in post or with film stock profiles that add the desired warmth or character
- Its sharpness can be demanding on technique — slight focus miss or camera movement are unforgiving
- Its perfection can feel cold to photographers who prefer optical character over technical excellence
- For medium format emulation on the M11 (60MP), this lens is the closest tool available
