# Leica Summilux-M 35mm f/1.4 ASPH — Lens Profile

**Full name:** Leica Summilux-M 35mm f/1.4 ASPH  
**Mount:** Leica M  
**Focal length:** 35mm  
**Maximum aperture:** f/1.4  
**Minimum aperture:** f/16  
**Minimum focus distance:** 0.7m  
**Construction:** 9 elements in 5 groups, 1 aspherical element  
**Era:** Current version (FLE): 2010–present | First ASPH: 1994–2010  
**FLE:** Floating Lens Element — corrects focus shift from f/1.4 to f/2.8  

---

## Visual Identity

The Summilux 35mm f/1.4 is the lens for photographers who need the 35mm environmental perspective but want the subject isolation capability of a fast aperture. It occupies a specific visual territory between the Summicron 35 (sharp, clean, moderate DOF) and the 50mm Summilux (narrower perspective, greater isolation).

At f/1.4, the 35mm Summilux produces a unique effect: the subject is isolated — especially at close focus — but the 35mm focal length means that the out-of-focus background is "closer" optically and therefore more present in the frame. This creates images where the subject is the clear point of attention but the environment is still legible, rather than completely dissolved as it would be at 50mm f/1.4.

The FLE (current) version solved a longstanding challenge: previous 35mm Summilux designs had noticeable focus shift when stopping down — the point of focus moved slightly as the aperture changed. The FLE version eliminates this, making the current Summilux 35 the most practically useful version.

---

## Rendering Character

**Wide open (f/1.4):** The Summilux 35 wide open has a pronounced character — a "drawing" quality with strong vignetting, slight glowing at edges of subjects, and background rendering that is busy compared to a 50mm at equivalent aperture. The 35mm's inherently wider depth of field means bokeh at the same aperture is less smooth than at 50mm or longer.

**At f/2:** The rendering settles. Vignetting reduces. Sharpness improves substantially. This is the street photographer's sweet spot — fast enough for available light, rendering that is clean and controlled.

**At f/2.8–f/4:** Excellent performance. Clean, high micro-contrast, corner-to-corner uniformity approaching the Summicron 35.

**Colour transmission:** Warm — warmer than the Summicron 35. The Summilux coating adds a rich, slightly golden quality to the rendering. Skin tones and natural light particularly benefit.

**Vignetting at f/1.4:** Strong — approximately 2.5 stops at extreme corners. This adds to the "Leica portrait in available light" look — frames naturally emphasise the centre, borders darken organically.

## Combination Notes

**Classic combination:** M body + Summilux 35 f/1.4 + Kodak Tri-X. Available-light street photography with deep grain and the drama of fast aperture rendering. The vignetting adds graphic power to the frame.

**Environmental portraits:** The 35mm perspective and f/1.4 aperture together allow portraits where the subject is the clear focus but their surroundings tell a story — a combination not achievable at 50mm.
