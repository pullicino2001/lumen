# Leica SL2 / SL2-S — Camera Profile

**Type:** Full-frame Mirrorless  
**Sensor:** SL2: 47.3MP CMOS | SL2-S: 24.6MP CMOS (BSI)  
**Production:** SL2: 2019–present | SL2-S: 2020–present  
**ISO Range:** SL2: 50–50,000 | SL2-S: 100–100,000  
**Autofocus:** Phase-detect AF (fast, accurate, subject tracking)  
**Lens Mount:** L-Mount (also accepts M-mount via adapter)  
**Significance:** Leica's professional mirrorless — used by commercial and editorial photographers who need Leica colour science with autofocus, weather sealing, and versatility.

---

## Visual Identity

The SL2 is Leica's answer to the question: "What if you wanted Leica colour but also needed to shoot a moving subject, or in the rain, or with a 400mm lens?" It is a large, weather-sealed professional body that competes directly with the Sony A7R IV and Nikon Z7 — but renders differently from both.

The SL2 shares the M10/Q2-generation Leica colour science. Where it differs from the M cameras is in its processing character: the SL2 is more aggressively corrected — lens distortion, vignetting, and chromatic aberration are all corrected in-camera when shooting with SL-mount lenses. The result is technically purer images with less of the natural optical character that M-mount lenses retain.

The SL2-S is the low-light and video-oriented variant. At 24MP, its larger pixels capture significantly better high-ISO performance. For portrait, event, and documentary work in difficult light, the SL2-S is the stronger choice.

---

## Colour Science

The SL2 uses the same Leica colour matrix philosophy as the M and Q cameras. The signature warmth in skin tones, rich reds, and clean blues is present. However:

- At 47MP, fine colour gradations are resolved with exceptional smoothness
- The electronic shutter enables silent shooting without vibration — no mirror, no mechanical noise
- Video output has the same colour science — the SL2-S is used professionally for cinematographic-quality video

---

## Rendering Character

**Sharpness:** At 47MP with SL-mount lenses, the SL2 is among the sharpest full-frame cameras made. Leica's SL-series lenses (Summilux-SL 50mm f/1.4, APO-Summicron-SL 28mm f/2) are designed to resolve every pixel.

**When used with M-mount lenses (via adapter):** Character changes significantly. M-mount lenses on the SL2 retain their natural optical behaviour — vignetting, slight distortion, corner softness. The combination of M-mount character optics on a 47MP sensor is remarkable: you get the full resolution of the SL2 sensor resolved through the natural, un-corrected rendering of classic M lenses.

**Dynamic range:** Excellent. The SL2's sensor provides over 14 stops of dynamic range — impressive shadow and highlight recovery.

**Noise character:** Fine and luminance-dominant. Less "analogue" than the M cameras' noise — more digital in character at high ISO.
