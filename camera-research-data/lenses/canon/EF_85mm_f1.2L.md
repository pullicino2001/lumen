# Canon EF 85mm f/1.2L II USM — Lens Profile

**Full name:** Canon EF 85mm f/1.2L II USM  
**Mount:** Canon EF  
**Focal length:** 85mm  
**Maximum aperture:** f/1.2  
**Minimum aperture:** f/16  
**Minimum focus distance:** 0.95m  
**Construction:** 8 elements in 7 groups  
**Era:** 2006–present (II version)  

---

## Visual Identity

The Canon 85mm f/1.2L is one of the most recognisable portrait lens looks in photography. It produces a specific aesthetic that defined high-end portrait and wedding photography for two decades: extremely narrow depth of field at f/1.2, smooth and creamy background dissolution, warm Canon colour science, and a slight "dreamy" quality from the residual spherical aberration wide open.

This lens is not about technical perfection. Wide open, the Canon 85L is soft — not "bad" soft, but a beautiful softness that flatters skin, dissolves backgrounds, and creates images with an almost ethereal quality. Stopped to f/1.8–f/2, it becomes very sharp while retaining its famous background rendering. At f/4+, it is one of the sharpest 85mm lenses available.

---

## Rendering Character

**At f/1.2:** Maximum expression of the Canon 85L look. Focus is very narrow and slightly "floaty" — subjects are sharp at the focus point but with a luminous glow that flatters skin. Bokeh is exceptionally smooth and circular.

**Colour:** Canon's warm colour science combined with the 85mm telephoto compression creates an extremely flattering portrait rendering. Skin tones are warm, smooth, and forgiving.

**Bokeh at f/1.2:** Circular, very smooth. The Canon 85L bokeh is one of the most praised in all of photography — backgrounds dissolve seamlessly, without nervous edges or concentric rings in bokeh circles.

**Vignetting:** Strong at f/1.2 — approximately 2 stops at corners. Creates natural portrait framing.

**Focus:** Slow autofocus (Canon's USM on this lens is slower than modern designs). The f/1.2 aperture requires precise focus — a slight focus miss is very visible at this depth of field.

## Combination Notes

**5D Mark IV + 85mm f/1.2L:** The classic event/wedding combination. Warm, beautiful, technically demanding. Images from this combination have a specific quality immediately recognisable to photographers.

**With Kodak Portra 400 simulation:** The Canon warmth + Portra warmth creates an almost excessively warm, romantic aesthetic — popular for certain types of lifestyle and wedding photography.
