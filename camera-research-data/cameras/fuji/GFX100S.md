# Fujifilm GFX 100S — Camera Profile

**Type:** Medium Format Mirrorless  
**Sensor:** 102MP GFX CMOS (43.8mm × 32.9mm — medium format)  
**Production:** 2021–present  
**ISO Range:** 100–12,800 (expandable to 51,200)  
**Lens Mount:** G-Mount  
**Significance:** The most accessible medium format digital camera. 102MP medium-format sensor in a body the size of a professional DSLR.

---

## Visual Identity

The GFX 100S is important for LUMEN because its medium-format sensor represents a rendering quality that the app can reference as a "medium format" aesthetic tier. When users want maximum quality, maximum tonal depth, maximum detail — this is the reference point.

Medium format differs from full-frame in ways that are genuinely visible in photographs:

**Tonal graduation:** The larger sensor captures more tonal steps between deep shadow and bright highlight. Transitions in sky, skin, and smooth surfaces are more continuous — less "digital steppy" and more "film smooth."

**Colour depth:** More bits of colour information per pixel. Subtle colour gradients in skin, fabric, and landscape are retained with greater fidelity.

**"Medium format look":** Difficult to define precisely but visible — images from medium format sensors have a quality of depth and dimensionality that is perceptibly different from full-frame at the same lens equivalence. Often described as "large print quality" even when viewed digitally.

**Depth of field:** A 50mm on medium format gives the same DOF as approximately 40mm on full-frame — slightly more depth of field than the equivalent focal length on full-frame, giving medium format images a quality of environmental sharpness combined with subject presence.
